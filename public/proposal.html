Strategy Proposal · May 2026

Aura: Glow-Up OS

Pivot Aura Divine away from generic dating into a self-upgrade OS for men 18–26. Looksmaxxing in, habits stay, dating coach pays.

1. Executive summary

You've built three products and shipped zero. The instinct to pivot to love · beauty · lean-bulk-to-slay is directionally right — that market is bigger and more monetizable than edtech (Qubit) or read-later (Pocket). But "love is forever, people will pay" is true for the category, false for any specific product. Generic dating apps are contracting; you can't beat Bumble at being Bumble, and you don't need to.

The bet that fits your stack: repurpose Aura Divine into a Glow-Up OS. Acquisition wedge = AI looksmaxxing scoring (TikTok-native, $50-creator playbook). Retention moat = your already-built habit tracker + XP system (the thing every Umax clone lacks). Monetization upsell = AI dating coach (Rizz-style screenshot reply). Reuse Pocket's Firebase/Cloud Functions/Vite-React stack wholesale.

One SKU at launch. One channel (TikTok creators). 90-day kill criterion: 1k WAU + $1k MRR or stop. No fourth product.

2. Honest pushback (read once)

The risk is not picking the wrong market — it's the abandonment pattern. "My desire dried out" is a founder-side problem; the next product gets the same treatment in 6 months unless you change how you commit, not what.

Two corrections to your framing: (1) "I have infra" — you have a frontend prototype + Pocket's Firebase scaffold. The new app is closer to a fresh build than you think. (2) Bigger markets are usually harder, not easier. Pick one wedge, set a kill criterion, ship. Don't pivot from this one too.

4. The bet: Glow-Up OS

One sentence: "Get hot. Track the glow-up. Get matches."

The four-step loop, none of which exists as a single product today:

  1. Acquisition (TikTok-native): AI face/body scoring with hard paywall reveal. Looksmaxxing wedge — viral, proven.
  2. Retention (the moat): Habit tracker + Aura XP you've already built. Skincare, gym, sleep, photos. Weekly "potential" rescore. This is what no Umax clone has — their D30 is brutal.
  3. Monetization (Pro tier): AI dating coach — paste a Hinge/Tinder screenshot, get reply suggestions. Higher willingness to pay than scoring.
  4. v2 add-on: AI dating-profile photo generator via Replicate / Fal API. Capital-light bolt-on once you have users.

Why this beats starting a fourth product

  • Reuses ~70% of the Aura React frontend (onboarding, habit tracker, XP, streaks, Profile, swipe component).
  • Reuses Pocket's Firebase + Cloud Functions stack wholesale (see Section 6).
  • Hits all three of your stated pulls: love (dating coach), beauty (looksmaxxing), lean bulk to slay (body recomp tracking).
  • Targets a real product gap. Umax owns step 1 only. Rizz owns step 3 only. PhotoAI owns step 4 only. Built With Science owns step 2 for fitness only. Nobody owns the whole loop.

7. 30 / 60 / 90 roadmap

Days 0–30 — ship the acquisition wedge

  • New Firebase project, lift Pocket's firebase.json + /functions scaffold.
  • Build looksmaxxing flow: selfie upload → Cloud Function → GPT-4o vision → score (jaw, skin, hair, "potential") → paywall reveal.
  • Migrate Aura's onboarding + habit tracker + XP behind paywall.
  • Wire RevenueCat, Apple/Google in-app purchases.
  • Single SKU: men 18–26.
  • Brand: keep Aura — gold + clean white UI you already redesigned reads premium.

Days 31–60 — distribution test

  • Gift 20 mid-tier TikTok creators ($50–150 each) the Anderson hook ("I rated my face with AI"). Track installs via App Store deep links.
  • Ship Pro tier with Rizz-style screenshot → reply (Cloud Function proxying GPT-4o).
  • ASO: target looksmax, glow up, rizz, dating coach.
  • Kill criterion: <5k installs after creator gifting + organic → stop creator spend, reassess hook.

Days 61–90 — retention & monetization

  • Weekly rescore cadence with photo-diff overlay.
  • Push notifications tied to streak (FCM, leverages existing XP system).
  • A/B test paywall (weekly vs monthly default; 3-day trial vs no trial).
  • Go/no-go gate at day 90: 1k WAU + $1k MRR + D30 ≥ 15%. Hit → raise paid UA budget. Miss → stop. Do not pivot to a fifth product.
